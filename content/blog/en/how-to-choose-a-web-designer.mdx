Choosing the right person or agency to build your website is one of the most important decisions you'll make.

A well-designed website should not only look great but also load quickly, rank well in Google, and help turn visitors into customers.

## Review their portfolio

The first thing you should check is previous work.

Pay attention to:

- design quality,
- loading speed,
- mobile responsiveness,
- variety of completed projects.

Quality is always more important than the number of projects.

## Understand what's included

Before you accept an offer, make sure it covers everything you need.

A professional website package should include:

- custom design,
- responsive layout,
- basic SEO,
- contact forms,
- deployment,
- post-launch support.

This helps avoid unexpected costs later.

## Communication matters

A good web designer will ask questions about your business, your customers, and your goals.

If someone gives you a fixed quote without understanding your project first, it's worth being cautious.

## Don't choose based on price alone

The cheapest offer isn't always the best value.

A poorly built website may require expensive fixes or even a complete redesign after only a short time.

Think of your website as an investment rather than a one-time expense.

## Questions worth asking

Before starting the project, ask:

- Will I have full access to my website?
- Can I edit the content myself?
- How long will the project take?
- Do you provide ongoing support?
- Will the website be optimized for SEO?

A good web designer is more than just someone who writes code.

The right partner will help you build a fast, modern website that supports your business for years to come.
