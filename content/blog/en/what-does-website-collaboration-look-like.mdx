Many people put off the decision to create a website because they don't know what to expect. Will it be complicated? Do I need to prepare a lot of materials? How long will it take? This article walks you through the entire process from start to finish.

## Step 1 — First enquiry

Everything starts with a short message. You fill in the contact form or write directly by email — describe what your business does and what you need. You don't have to know everything — a general idea is enough.

Within 1–2 business days, I'll get back to you with questions and an initial quote.

## Step 2 — Brief and direction

If we decide to work together, we fill in a short brief. It covers a few questions about:

- The goal of the site and your target audience
- Visual style (do you have a logo? What colours do you like?)
- Inspiration — websites you find appealing
- Features you need

The brief usually takes 20–30 minutes and gives me everything I need to get started.

## Step 3 — Design and development

Based on the brief, I create the website design. First, I show you the layout as a mockup or finished design — you can give feedback and request changes.

Once the design is approved, I move on to coding. I build the site in WordPress or Next.js, ensuring it's responsive, fast, and SEO-optimised.

## Step 4 — Testing and revisions

Before launch, I test the site across different devices and browsers. You also get time to review everything and flag any changes.

The standard package includes **two rounds of revisions**.

## Step 5 — Launch and handover

The site goes live on your hosting, or I set it up for you. I show you how to use the admin panel (if it's WordPress) and how to make basic changes.

I also provide brief documentation and am happy to answer any questions.

## How long does the whole process take?

A standard project takes **7–14 working days** from design approval. The timeline depends on the complexity of the site and how quickly you respond to my questions.

## What do you need to prepare?

- Content for the site (texts, service descriptions) — I can help you shape them
- Photos or access to a stock photo library
- Logo (if you have one)
- Company details for the footer and contact section

The whole process is simple and stress-free — I'm here to help you at every stage.
