Building a website is usually a one-time investment, but there are also ongoing costs to consider. **How much does it cost to maintain a website each year?** For a simple business website, the cost can often be just a few hundred złoty or the equivalent in your local currency.

Let's take a look at what you actually need to pay for and which costs are worth considering.

## Domain Name

A domain is the address of your website, for example `yourbusiness.com`.

It is usually renewed once a year. The first year can often be very inexpensive, so it is important to check the **renewal price**, not just the introductory offer.

Depending on the provider and domain extension, the annual cost can vary significantly.

## Web Hosting

Hosting is the server space where your website is stored.

For a small business website, you usually don't need an expensive server. Basic hosting can be relatively inexpensive and is normally paid monthly or annually.

The price depends on factors such as:

* server performance
* number of websites
* available storage
* backups
* quality of technical support

Online stores and larger websites with more traffic may require more powerful and therefore more expensive hosting.

## SSL Certificate

An SSL certificate provides a secure, encrypted connection between your website and its visitors. It is also what allows your website address to start with `https://`.

Today, many hosting providers include a **free SSL certificate**, so most small business websites do not need to pay extra for it.

## Updates and Security

Website maintenance costs also depend on the technology used to build your website.

### WordPress

WordPress requires regular updates, including:

* WordPress core
* plugins
* themes
* security updates

An outdated website can become more vulnerable to security issues and technical problems.

### Next.js

Websites built with Next.js do not require traditional WordPress hosting or regular updates to multiple plugins.

Simple projects can also be deployed on platforms such as Vercel, which can keep infrastructure costs very low for smaller websites.

However, project dependencies should still be updated periodically, and the website should be checked to make sure everything continues to work correctly.

## Technical Support

Not every website requires ongoing technical support.

However, if you need regular changes, it may be useful to work with a web developer who can take care of your website.

Technical support may include:

* updating content
* adding new sections
* creating backups
* system updates
* fixing technical issues
* improving website performance

Support can be billed monthly or hourly depending on your business needs.

## How Much Does It Cost to Maintain a Simple Website?

For a small business website, the main costs usually include:

### Domain — Annual Cost

Your website address, usually renewed once per year.

### Hosting — Monthly or Annual Cost

The server required to keep your website online. The price depends on the hosting provider and the resources your website needs.

### SSL — Often Free

Many hosting providers include a free SSL certificate as part of their hosting package.

### Technical Support — Optional

If you need regular updates, changes or technical assistance, you should also consider the cost of professional support.

## Do You Have to Pay for a Website Every Month?

**Not always.**

For a simple business website, the main recurring expenses may only be the domain and hosting.

Additional monthly costs usually appear when you use paid tools, third-party integrations, premium services or ongoing technical support.

## What Should You Not Save Money On?

The cheapest option is not always the best one.

When maintaining a business website, pay particular attention to:

* security
* website speed
* backups
* server reliability
* technical support

Your website should remain fast, secure and available to potential customers — especially if it is an important source of leads for your business.

## Final Thoughts

Website maintenance **doesn't have to be expensive**. For a simple business website, the main ongoing expenses are usually the domain and hosting.

Before starting a new website project, it is worth asking not only **"How much does it cost to build a website?"** but also **"How much will it cost to maintain it?"**
